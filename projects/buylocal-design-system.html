The Design System

The design system grew out of the user research done for BuyLocal. After interviewing 10 users in two countries and observing how they shop in context, three things stood out: people want to shop efficiently and conveniently, they compare price and quality before buying, and they want local stores to be visible online. The system encodes these insights into a shared set of tokens, components, and patterns.

Store and product cards present price, discounts, and availability in a consistent way, so customers can compare offers quickly across multiple nearby stores. The checkout and order tracking flows were shaped by the shipping and delivery concerns raised in the interviews, with clear states for no-contact delivery and live order updates. A compact, mobile-first layout keeps the steps between browsing and paying to a minimum, matching the efficiency participants asked for.

The component set covers both core tasks from the research, buying fresh products from a supermarket and ordering non-perishable products online, including the discount and premium subscription flows. Defining these patterns once kept the app consistent across screens and made it faster to design new features.
